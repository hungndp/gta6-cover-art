About this app

Neon Vice Cover Studio builds GTA 6-style poster covers from your own photos. Fonts Anton, Archivo Black and Oswald ship under the SIL Open Font License (see app/fonts/OFL.txt). No Rockstar artwork, logo or font is used. Works fully offline — no network requests, no uploads.
