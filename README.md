**Neon Vice Cover Studio** — a GTA 6-style multi-panel poster cover maker. Turn your own photos into a Vice City poster: 8 layout templates (2–8 panels, including a Mosaic 8-panel layout), 8 colour grades (Sunset and Neon flagships) with a strength slider, face-aware crops with draggable focus points and skin-tone auto-detect, cast role tags (LEAD / CREW / RIVAL / DRIVER / LOOKOUT / ENFORCER / FIXER / WHEELMAN + custom), annotation callouts with leader lines, a Vice City seal stamp, an editable billing block, 5 caption styles with auto-fit sizing, and PNG export in 1080×1080, 1080×1350 and 1920×1080. Fully offline — no network, no uploads, no Rockstar artwork or fonts. Fonts Anton, Archivo Black and Oswald ship under the SIL Open Font License (see `app/fonts/OFL.txt`).

## Run it

```sh
cd app && python3 -m http.server 8080
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. Upload a photo (or use a sample), pick a layout and grade, drag the focus point on any panel, set role tags, callouts, seal and billing, and export your cover as a PNG.
