Neon Vice Cover Studio — turn your photos into a GTA 6-style multi-panel poster cover. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's "Make GTA 6 Cover Art" bounty, $250 in USDC.

Pick a layout (8 templates, 2–8 panels including a Mosaic 8-panel poster), a colour grade (8 grades — Sunset and Neon are the flagships — with a strength slider), tag your cast (LEAD, RIVAL, DRIVER, LOOKOUT and more, plus custom tags), let the face-aware crops find your subjects, add callouts with leader lines, stamp the Vice City seal, write your billing credits, and export a PNG in 1080×1080, 1080×1350 or 1920×1080 — all offline, nothing uploaded. Fonts are Bebas Neue, Barlow Condensed Bold and Montserrat, all under the SIL Open Font License. No Rockstar Games logo, artwork, character or font is used.

## Run it

```sh
python3 -m http.server 8080 --directory app
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — load a photo (or use a sample), pick a layout and grade, and export your cover. Any static file server works; the app needs no build step and no network.
